About me

Hi! I'm Robbie. I'm a developer and an artist (slowly?) making a bunch of stuff.

Interests, goals

I want to make great commercial open-source video games with homebrew support. Broadly speaking, I think it's really cool to write software with high standards for compatibility and performance.

You can expect most of my personal work, both code and art, to be under a copyleft license, a permissive license, or a combination of the two. I want my work to be accessible to everyone, free to be modified and shared as the user sees fit.

My current project is a 2D pixel art game in C for PC and PlayStation Portable. With everything going to plan, I'll have more to share with you in the coming months!

Besides software development, I also make digital art and post it on Bluesky and X. It's mostly Bee & PuppyCat fanart (I love the show).
